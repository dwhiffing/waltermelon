# waltermelon

- tap z to jump, hold it to jump higher
- press left/right to move
- hold up/down to look
- press down to crouch/roll
- move into ledges to grab/hoist
- watch out for slopes, you can slide down them if you approach them slowly enough, but otherwise, you'll start rolling
- collect all 50 watermelons
- press m to toggle between: mute music, mute sound + music, mute off
